Privacy Policy

Reef Tank collects no personal information. It has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Who we are. Reef Tank is a screensaver for Roku devices, developed by Luke G. ("we", "us").

Information we collect. None. Reef Tank does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information, viewing history, device identifiers or usage data.

Settings stored on your device. Your Reef Tank settings (day and night mode, number of fish, and visitor preferences) are saved only on your Roku device, in storage that Roku provides to each app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Removing Reef Tank from your Roku deletes them.

No internet connection. Everything Reef Tank shows is included in the app. It makes no network requests while running.

Roku. Reef Tank runs on the Roku platform. Roku's own collection and use of data is governed by Roku's privacy policy, not this one.

Children. Reef Tank is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
