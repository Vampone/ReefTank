Common questions

How do I set Reef Tank as my screensaver?

On your Roku, go to Settings, then Theme, then Screensavers, and choose Reef Tank.

How do I change the settings?

On your Roku, go to Settings, then Theme, then Screensavers, select Reef Tank, and choose Change screensaver settings.

The tank looks dark. Is something wrong?

Reef Tank follows your Roku's clock, so after dark it switches to moonlight. To keep it bright, set Day & night to Always day.
